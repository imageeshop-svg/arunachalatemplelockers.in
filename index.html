How it works

Four simple steps — online or at our shop.

  1. 1

    Choose a free locker

    On this website, in our Android app, or at our shop counter.

  2. 2

    Pay by UPI

    Scan the QR code or tap the UPI button. An online booking holds your locker for 15 minutes while you pay.

  3. 3

    Check in at the shop

    Show your booking reference at the counter before 9 PM. We open your locker and your 10 hours start. You get a WhatsApp receipt.

  4. 4

    Collect your luggage

    Come back to the counter to check out. Pay any extra hours there. You get a WhatsApp check-out receipt.

Locker rules

  • Lockers are for short-term storage of personal luggage, helmets and mobile phones.
  • Do not store illegal, dangerous, flammable or perishable items.
  • Please do not leave cash, jewellery or other valuables in the locker.

Frequently asked questions

Where are the lockers?
At our shop, 52 A Car Street, Tiruvannamalai – 606601, near Arunachaleswarar Temple. Use the “Get directions” button for Google Maps.
What are your opening hours?
Every day, 7 AM – 9 PM. Online booking is also open only from 7 AM to 9 PM.
How much does it cost?
₹50 for a medium bag or a helmet, ₹100 for a jumbo suitcase — per item, for 10 hours. After 10 hours, ₹10 for each extra hour (part of an hour counts as a full hour), paid at checkout.
When do my 10 hours start?
When your locker is opened for you at the shop (check-in) — not when you pay.
How long is a locker held while I pay online?
15 minutes. If the payment is not completed in time, the booking expires and the locker is released. If money was deducted, call 88380 75040 with your booking reference — the shop will confirm your booking or refund you.
By when must I check in after paying?
On the same day, before the shop closes at 9 PM. If you are late, call 88380 75040 with your booking reference.
Can I keep a jumbo suitcase?
Yes — ₹100 per jumbo suitcase. It needs a Large (L) or Extra large (XL) locker, so choose an L or XL locker on the map.
How many items can I book at once?
Up to 6 items (bags, helmets and jumbo suitcases together) in one booking.
How do I pay?
By UPI — Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm. Scan the QR code or tap the UPI button on the booking page. Extra hours are paid at the counter when you collect.
Will I get a receipt?
Yes. We send your receipt on WhatsApp to the mobile number you give — when you check in and when you check out.
What can I not keep in the locker?
Illegal, dangerous, flammable or perishable items. Please do not leave cash, jewellery or other valuables.
Can I get a refund?
No refund once a locker has been assigned or used. If money was deducted but no locker was given, the full amount is refunded within 5–7 working days.
I lost my booking reference. What do I do?
Call 88380 75040 or come to the counter with the mobile number you used for the booking.
Is there an app for iPhone?
Not yet. iPhone users can book on this website. Android users can download our app above.
