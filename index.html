How it works

Book online, on the app or at the shop – registration is not needed.

  1. 1

    Choose a free locker

    On this website or our Android app, tap a white (free) locker and enter your name, mobile number and items. Or just walk in to the shop.

  2. 2

    Pay by UPI

    Pay by UPI – Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm. Your locker is held for you after payment.

  3. 3

    Check in at the shop

    Come to 52 A Car Street. Staff note the last 4 digits of your Aadhaar and open your locker. Your 10 hours start now and you get a WhatsApp receipt.

  4. 4

    Collect your luggage

    Come back to the counter to collect. Pay any extra hours there. You get a WhatsApp check-out receipt.

Locker rules

  • Lockers are for short-term storage of personal luggage, helmets and mobile phones.
  • Do not store illegal, dangerous, flammable or perishable items.
  • Please do not leave cash, jewellery or other valuables in the locker.
  • Charges are as listed above. Extra hours are payable at checkout.
  • Staff note the last 4 digits of your Aadhaar at check-in. Registered regular visitors: staff also check your saved photo at check-in and check-out.

Frequently asked questions

Where are the lockers?
At our shop, 52 A Car Street, Tiruvannamalai – 606601, near Arunachaleswarar Temple. Use the “Get directions” button for Google Maps.
What are your opening hours?
Every day, 7 AM – 9 PM. You can book on this website or the app at any time and check in at the shop during opening hours.
How much does it cost?
₹50 for a medium bag or a helmet, ₹100 for a jumbo suitcase — per item, for 10 hours. After 10 hours, ₹10 for each extra hour (part of an hour counts as a full hour), paid at checkout.
When do my 10 hours start?
When your locker is opened for you at the shop (check-in) — not when you pay.
Do I need to register?
No. Registration is optional. It is for regular visitors: send your details once on this website or the app, bring your Aadhaar card on your next visit, and staff approve you. After that you are served faster at the counter.
Can I book or pay online?
Yes. Book a free locker on this website or the Android app and pay by UPI. Then come to the shop to check in – your 10 hours start when staff open your locker.
Can I keep a jumbo suitcase?
Yes – ₹100 per jumbo suitcase. It needs a Large (L) or Extra large (XL) locker; staff choose one for you at the shop.
How many items can I book at once?
Up to 6 items (bags, helmets and jumbo suitcases together) in one booking.
How do I pay?
By UPI – Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm – online when you book, or at the shop counter. Extra hours are paid at the counter when you collect.
Will I get a receipt?
Yes. We send your receipt on WhatsApp to the mobile number you give — when you check in and when you check out.
What can I not keep in the locker?
Illegal, dangerous, flammable or perishable items. Please do not leave cash, jewellery or other valuables.
Can I get a refund?
No refund once a locker has been assigned or used. If money was deducted but no locker was given, the full amount is refunded within 5–7 working days.
I forgot my booking reference. What do I do?
Tell your mobile number at the counter, or call 88380 75040. Staff will find your booking.
Is there an app for iPhone?
Not yet. iPhone users can book on this website. Android users can download our app above.

Policies

Refund policy

  • No refund once a locker has been assigned or used.
  • If money was deducted but no locker was given, the full amount will be refunded within 5–7 working days.

Privacy policy

For the locker service we keep your name and mobile (WhatsApp) number. At check-in staff note the last 4 digits of your Aadhaar number – the card image or QR code is never kept. If you choose to register as a regular visitor, we also keep your address and a recent photo; only authorised shop staff can see them, and your Aadhaar card is shown to staff only to compare its photo with you. We use your details only to give you a locker, contact you about your booking and keep business records. We do not sell or share your details with anyone, except where required by law.
